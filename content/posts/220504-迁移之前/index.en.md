Back in college I had a WordPress site hosted on Alibaba Cloud that held my archive of old posts; at a bit over 100 yuan a year (roughly $15), the price was acceptable. But after graduation, on one hand I had less appetite for tinkering and couldn't manage more than a few posts a year; on the other hand, the renewal price jumped straight to around 1,300 yuan (about $180). Considering that return on investment, I shut the old site down.

But **programming and writing are the most easily reached levers ordinary people have in our era** — so I should still try hard to keep producing.

As a lazy person, my precondition for sticking with anything is being able to push its barrier to entry as low as possible. Hexo is nice, but for someone like me who only occasionally writes code, it was still a bit too complicated, and it bruised my motivation to write.

I chose Gridea precisely because it's simple enough — there's a client app and you can just start writing. It wasn't without reservations, though: the author's last update was back in 2020.10, and I'm somewhat worried that no one will maintain it down the road.

But, anyway — write first, figure the rest out later.

This blog is meant to hold tinkerings unrelated to work, and the old historical posts will be migrated over gradually.
