A few days ago, following [Several Ways for Chinese Citizens to Apply for a US EIN — VPS Dawanjia](https://www.vpsdawanjia.com/4450.html) (a Chinese blog), I applied for an EIN by phone. Here is a record of the process.

Although I have an ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number), applying for an EIN (Employer Identification Number) actually requires neither an ITIN nor founding a company.

## ITIN — the online application: possible in principle, not in practice

Guided by the principle of "if it can be solved online, don't make a phone call," I first tried applying for the EIN by filling in the online form.

I opened the [Apply for an Employer Identification Number (EIN) Online | Internal Revenue Service](https://www.irs.gov/zh-hans/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online) page and followed the flow.

Here I did not run into the **error message after entering the ITIN** that the Dawanjia post mentioned — after entering my ITIN I proceeded normally to the next step of filling in "Address," but this is where it got frustrating, because the form asks:

> Where is the Sole Proprietor physically located?

![The online application only accepts a US address](/migrated-images/picgo_c63889c792f2aaccd7799dac008f790.png)

I, the ITIN holder, am physically in China, yet the form only accepts a US address here.

Entering a rented US private mailbox address would indeed let me proceed to the next step, but to avoid trouble down the road, I gave up on the online application.


## Applying for the EIN by phone

The call took 30 minutes in total. Here I'd recommend the Chrome + Google Voice approach, for two reasons:

-   With GV (Google Voice) it costs nothing
-   Chrome supports Live Caption + Live Translate; the real-time speech-to-text is fairly accurate and effectively lowers the communication barrier (method at the end of this post).

The agent who answered was a very patient lady. In the middle, while filling in the address, she couldn't make out the Chinese address I was translating for her, so she offered on her own to bring a translator into the call.

If you don't feel confident, you can just tell her right at the start: "I want a Chinese translator."

### The main questions

-   what is your position with the business? : the owner and the responsible party of the business.
-   name (needs to be spelled out)
-   phone
-   Is this application for a limited liability company or its foreign equivalent nowadays? : No
-   If have SSN or ITIN?
-   Address & Birth Date
-   And do you need a trade name or doing business of things?
-   Mailing Address
-   start date of business
-   are you expecting to have any U. S. Employees in the next twelve months?
-   the principal activity of the business.?
-   will this involve any U. S. Gambling or wagering, any form twenty two ninety heavy highway use tax or form seven twenty export like character?
-   Do you want to use the same phone number from earlier as your business phone number, your U. S. Business phone numbers?

### Spelling tricks (to make sure nothing gets confused, e.g. the pronunciation of g and j)

When spelling out letters, it's best to use the pattern below to make sure the other party understands.

When you need to make sure the other party correctly understands the letter "W", you can dictate it as follows:

"W" as in whiskey,  
"A" as in alpha,  
"N" as in November,  
"G" as in golf.

That way it will be much easier for the other party to understand that the letter you mean is "W".

A few more English names for symbols:

-   dash "-"
-   comma ","
-   space " "

### Enabling real-time speech-to-text & translation in Chrome

If you only need real-time speech-to-text and not translation, any Chrome can do it.

[Use "Live Caption" in Chrome - Google Chrome Help](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/10538231?hl=zh-Hans)

If you need real-time translation, it is currently only available on the Chrome Canary channel.

-   Download [Chrome Canary Features For Developers - Google Chrome](https://www.google.com/intl/en_sg/chrome/canary/)
-   In the shortcut's properties, append the parameter

```
"C:\Users\zlx\AppData\Local\Google\Chrome SxS\Application\chrome.exe" --enable-features=LiveCaptionMultiLanguage,LiveTranslate
```
