## 1 Background on Chinese E-Textbooks

Chinese textbooks are not digitized as well as English ones. English textbooks are mostly output directly from source documents, while Chinese textbooks are mostly obtained by scanning printed books. That's why, for the same book, the English PDF may be only a few MB, while the Chinese translation can balloon to over a hundred MB. Also, a scanned PDF that hasn't gone through OCR doesn't support operations on its text.

Most Chinese PDF textbooks on the market come from one of four sources: **leaked library scans, personal scans uploaded by individuals, open-source books (rare), and leaked files from publishers (rare)**.

Once you know the sources, you can tailor your approach. Here's how to find them. 👀

PS: Textbooks in Word, mobi, or Epub formats can also be converted to PDF with the right software. For details, see [Can't tell mobi / epub / azw3 book formats apart?](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/0Ffajimw0UEqF7FS1pxXcg)

## 2 Ways to Get Chinese PDF Textbooks

### 2.1 Cloud Drive Search Engines

File sharing in China mostly relies on cloud drives, and textbooks are no exception. Besides **Baidu Netdisk**, these include a host of other cloud storage providers such as **Weipan (Sina), Lanzou Cloud, and Chengtong Netdisk**.

But most of these drives don't offer a search function. Here, "search" means searching **non-publicly shared** content. Take Baidu Netdisk as an example: publicly shared files are ones we can search directly. But for shares that come with an extraction code, the official service provides no such search entry point.

This has given rise to a whole batch of cloud drive search engines, such as [Cilimao](https://www.cilimao.cc/) and [Xilinjie](http://www.xilinjie.com/). There are many more sites like this; just Google it and you'll find plenty.

### 2.3 Buying from Professionals

#### 2.3.1 Check Whether the Library Has Scanned the Book and How Clear the Scan Is

As mentioned above, one source of Chinese PDF textbooks is leaked library scans, so you can also get the relevant PDF books by going to people with administrative access.

![mark](/migrated-images/markdown_20190324_X13RxyVEiAj4.png)

First, go to the [National Library Reference Consultation Alliance](http://www.ucdrs.superlib.net/) to look up the book. You can search by title, ISBN, author, and more (searching by ISBN is recommended, as it is very efficient).

PS: The ISBN is the number next to the barcode at the bottom right of the back cover. You can also find it on e-commerce product detail pages.

![mark](/migrated-images/markdown_20190324_c5fOJuC9Mit7.png)

If the top right of the [book detail page](http://t.cn/EJUWhDk) shows a **preview** (copyright page, preface, table of contents, main text), you can buy it this way. **If there is no preview, you cannot buy it this way.**

Click in and check the clarity: **the clarity of the preview is the clarity of the PDF textbook that gets produced**.

![mark](/migrated-images/markdown_20190324_pIbIJXaCK1Yq.png)

#### 2.3.2 Finding Someone to Buy From

You can find such people through channels like Taobao. A book usually costs 5-15 yuan; pick one yourself.

To avoid any suspicion of advertising, I won't recommend anyone in the article. But you can reply "**e-book purchase**" in the backend of the account to get the contact information of a practitioner I recommend, at about 3 yuan per book.

PS: The library's scan format is PDG, and converting it to PDF takes some time, so please be patient.

### 2.4 Searching with General Search Engines

If you've gotten to this step, then either your book is fairly new and the library hasn't gotten around to scanning it, or it's fairly obscure and demand is low.

So we have to rely on the vast community of netizens, using general search engines. I suggest Google or its [mirror sites](https://coderschool.cn/1853.html).

Of course, there are techniques to searching, too.

#### 2.4.1 Make Good Use of "filetype:"

![mark](/migrated-images/markdown_20190324_oV1lkzF9OMNI.png)

**Note**: **the colon is an English colon**

filetype means showing only a certain file type, such as .doc .pdf .xls .ppt .txt

#### 2.4.2 Make Good Use of English Double Quotes ""

![mark](/migrated-images/markdown_20190324_4BDSEXtbj4wR.png)

The content inside double quotes is **matched exactly**, meaning the result must contain exactly the same content as inside the quotes. It is very handy when searching among many similar books.

#### 2.4.3 Make Good Use of "-"

![mark](/migrated-images/markdown_20190324_TiTrd3my8uy2.png)

The minus sign is usually used to **exclude certain search content**. The figure shows the results for machine learning without python.

**Note: there must be a space between the preceding keyword and the minus sign, and there must be no space between the minus sign and the following keyword**

#### 2.4.4 More Search Syntax Tips

There are many more search syntax tricks, which I won't expand on here. You can refer to [this article](https://www.w3h5.com/post/220.html) to learn about them. Of course, knowing just a few common ones will greatly improve your search efficiency.

PS: Most search engines today are **keyword-based** rather than semantic, so please don't type "how to find the book Learn Python the Hard Way" 🙃🙃🙃. Instead, split it into keywords: "笨方法 python".

### 2.6 Crowdfunded Scanning

If you've tried all the methods above and still ended up here, congratulations: you're about to unlock a new skill. 😬

Please scan it yourself...

Buy the book on Taobao, ship it to a scanning shop on Taobao, and get a PDF.

Typical pricing:

> Pricing: 0.1 yuan/page for scanning with the book taken apart, 0.2 yuan/page for scanning without taking it apart. A3 scanning requires taking the book apart, at 0.2 yuan/page. If your book has 300 pages, taking it apart costs 30 yuan.

400 dpi is clear enough. I recommend scanning with the book taken apart, as the result is very good.

If you truly need it, I strongly suggest **starting a crowdfunded scan** for the book to split the cost. A somewhat thick book might sell for 15 yuan, but the whole service of scanning it into a PDF can easily exceed 100 yuan.

You can join the "**E-Textbook Project**" mentioned later to start your crowdfunding request (first judge for yourself whether the demand is niche).

## 3 Advanced Tips

### 3.1 About Unthrottled Baidu Netdisk

![mark](/migrated-images/markdown_20190324_ijecEGV84mbe.png)

There are many projects on GitHub for unthrottled Baidu Netdisk downloads, such as [ProxyeeDown](https://github.com/proxyee-down-org/proxyee-down); you can search for them yourself.

There are also some relatively mature third-party programs on the market, such as [Speedpan](https://www.speedpan.com/) and [PanDownload](https://pandownload.com/).

Of course, third-party clients all carry risk, and your account may get throttled.

You can also use IDM + Tampermonkey scripts, which is safer. If you're too lazy to tinker, I recommend [Naolao's method](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/q-hCL2Xm4AFb3vINDDw7gQ).
