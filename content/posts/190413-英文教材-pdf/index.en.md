## 1 Background on English E-Textbooks

Unlike Chinese textbooks, English textbooks are highly digitized. Most are exported directly from source documents, so they are **small in size and high in quality**. Chinese e-textbooks, by contrast, are mostly scanned copies of printed books: large in size and poorly suited to searching.

Because the two come from different sources, the approach to searching also differs.

English textbooks usually turn up best on dedicated e-book resource sites. A general-purpose search engine like Google is actually not the best choice here.

PS: Textbooks you find in Word, mobi, or Epub formats can also be converted to PDF with the right software. For the specific method, see [Can't tell mobi / epub / azw3 book formats apart?](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/0Ffajimw0UEqF7FS1pxXcg)

## 2 Ways to Get English E-Textbooks

### 2.1 [Z-library](https://zlibrary.jimdo.com/) Sites

> Z-Library is one of the largest online libraries in the world. We aim to make literature accessible for everyone. Today, Z-Library contains over 3 400 000 books and 73 000 000 articles.
> 
> Z-Library has many servers in the USA, Panama, Russia, Germany, Finland, Malaysia and Luxembourg. Our stored data now totals more than 160 TB! Every month, millions of people use Z-Library for their purposes — this shows us that we are on the right track. But it will be difficult to achieve our goals without your help. Z-Library is a non-profit project that exists at its founder’s expense, and with your support. Only thanks to your support over the past six months, we’ve made many improvements: full-text search, ZAlerts, recommender system, download history, improved advanced search and much more.

The above is the introduction to the Z-Library project. Its collection is extensive, with books in PDF, DjVu, and EPUB formats available for free. However, you can download a second book only after the first one has finished downloading.

The project has many mirror sites (after all, it threatens a lot of vested interests), such as [Booksc](https://booksc.org/), [Bookok](https://b-ok.org), and [BookFi](http://en.bookfi.net/g/).

PS: Besides textbooks, you can also download other e-books and articles. If the download speed is too slow, consider using Baidu Netdisk's offline download feature.

### 2.2 Library Genesis / LibGen Sites

> **Library Genesis** or **LibGen** is a search engine for articles and books on various topics,\[[1\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote1)which allows free access to content that is otherwise [paywalled](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Paywall) or not digitized elsewhere.\[[2\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote2) Among others, it carries [PDFs](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/PDF) of content from [Elsevier](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Elsevier)'s [ScienceDirect](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/ScienceDirect)web-portal.\[[3\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote03)
> 
> In 2015, the website became involved in a legal case when Elsevier accused it of providing [pirate](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Copyright_infringement) access to articles and books.\[[3\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote03) LibGen is reported to be registered in both Russia and Amsterdam, making it unclear which legislation applies,\[[3\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote03)\[[4\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote4) and whether defendants will show up in a United States court hearing.\[[3\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote03) LibGen is blocked by a number of [ISPs](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Internet_service_provider) in the United Kingdom,\[[5\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote5) but such [DNS](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Domain_Name_System)\-based blocks are claimed to do little to deter access.\[[3\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote03) In late October 2015, the [District Court for the Southern District of New York](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/United_States_District_Court_for_the_Southern_District_of_New_York) ordered LibGen to shut down and to suspend use of the domain name (libgen.org),\[[6\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote6) but the site is accessible through alternate domains.\[[7\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote7)\[[8\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote8)

### 2.5 Searching with General-Purpose Search Engines

If you've reached this step, then either your book is fairly new and the big sites haven't picked it up, or it's fairly obscure and very few people need it.

But the internet is huge, and the power of netizens is boundless. This is when we need a general-purpose search engine. I recommend Google or one of its [mirror sites](https://coderschool.cn/1853.html).

Of course, there are techniques to searching too.

#### 2.4.1 Make Good Use of "filetype:"

![](/migrated-images/markdown_20190324_oV1lkzF9OMNI.png)

**Note**: **the colon is an English colon**

filetype shows only results of a certain file type, such as .doc .pdf .xls .ppt .txt

#### 2.4.2 Make Good Use of English Double Quotes ""

![](/migrated-images/markdown_20190324_4BDSEXtbj4wR.png)

What's inside the double quotes is an **exact match**, meaning the result must contain exactly the same content as inside the quotes. This is handy when searching among many similar books.

#### 2.4.3 Make Good Use of "-"

![](/migrated-images/markdown_20190324_TiTrd3my8uy2.png)

The minus sign is usually used to **exclude certain search content**. The figure shows the results for machine learning searches that don't contain python.

**Note: there must be a space between the preceding keyword and the minus sign, and no space between the minus sign and the following keyword.**

#### 2.4.4 More Search Syntax Tips

There are many more search syntax tricks, which I won't expand on here. You can refer to [this article](https://www.w3h5.com/post/220.html) to learn about them. Of course, knowing just a few common ones can greatly improve your search efficiency.

PS: Most search engines today are **keyword-based rather than semantic**, so don't type "how to find the book Learn Python the Hard Way" 🙃🙃🙃. Instead, break it into keywords, such as "Learn Python the Hard Way python".

Why do we need the "Mastering PDFs" series?

First, with iPads and Surface devices becoming increasingly popular, more and more people are choosing digital ways of learning. But many people either can't find digital study materials, or find materials of poor quality, which greatly **hurts the efficiency of digital learning**.
