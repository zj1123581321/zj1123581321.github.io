## 1. Background: English E-Textbooks

Unlike Chinese textbooks, English textbooks are highly digitized. Most are exported directly from the source documents — **small in size, high in quality**. Chinese e-textbooks, on the other hand, are mostly scanned photocopies of the printed book: large files, poor for searching.

They come from different sources, so the search strategies differ too.

English textbooks usually give the best search results on dedicated e-book resource sites. General-purpose search engines like Google are actually not the best choice here.

PS: textbooks found in Word, mobi, Epub and other formats can be converted to PDF with the right software; see [mobi / epub / azw3 — can't tell the book formats apart?](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/0Ffajimw0UEqF7FS1pxXcg) (Chinese) for the specifics.

> Z-Library has many servers in the USA, Panama, Russia, Germany, Finland, Malaysia and Luxembourg. Our stored data now totals more than 160 TB! Every month, millions of people use Z-Library for their purposes — this shows us that we are on the right track. But it will be difficult to achieve our goals without your help. That's the project's own introduction. The book collection is extremely rich — pdf, djvu, epub formats all available, free to download. The catch: you have to finish downloading one book before you can download the next.

The project has many mirror sites (it did step on plenty of toes, after all), such as [Booksc](https://booksc.org/), [Bookok](https://b-ok.org), [BookFi](http://en.bookfi.net/g/).

> 
> In 2015, the website became involved in a legal case when Elsevier accused it of providing [pirate](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Copyright_infringement) access to articles and books.\[[3\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote03) LibGen is reported to be registered in both Russia and Amsterdam, making it unclear which legislation applies,\[[3\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote03)\[[4\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote4) and whether defendants will show up in a United States court hearing.\[[3\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote03) LibGen is blocked by a number of [ISPs](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Internet_service_provider) in the United Kingdom,\[[5\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote5) but such [DNS](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Domain_Name_System)\-based blocks are claimed to do little to deter access.\[[3\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote03) In late October 2015, the [District Court for the Southern District of New York](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/United_States_District_Court_for_the_Southern_District_of_New_York) ordered LibGen to shut down and to suspend use of the domain name (libgen.org),\[[6\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote6) but the site is accessible through alternate domains.\[[7\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote7)\[[8\]](https://www.wikiwand.com/en/Library_Genesis#citenote8)

### 2.5 General Search Engines

If you've made it this far, either your book is too new for the big sites to have indexed it, or it's too obscure — hardly anyone is looking for it.

But the internet is vast, and the power of the crowd is limitless. This is when we turn to general search engines. I recommend Google or one of its [mirror sites](https://coderschool.cn/1853.html) (for readers where Google is blocked).

And of course, searching has its own techniques.

#### 2.4.1 Make Good Use of "filetype:"

![](/migrated-images/markdown_20190324_oV1lkzF9OMNI.png)

**Note**: **the colon must be the English (half-width) colon**

filetype restricts results to a single file type, e.g. .doc .pdf .xls .ppt .txt

#### 2.4.2 Make Good Use of English Double Quotes ""

![](/migrated-images/markdown_20190324_4BDSEXtbj4wR.png)

Content inside double quotes is **matched exactly** — meaning the page must contain the exact content inside the quotes. Very handy when searching among many similar books.

#### 2.4.3 Make Good Use of "-"

![](/migrated-images/markdown_20190324_TiTrd3my8uy2.png)

The minus sign is usually used to **exclude a search term**; the screenshot shows search results for machine learning excluding python.

**Note: there must be a space between the preceding keyword and the minus sign, and no space between the minus sign and the following keyword**

#### 2.4.4 More Search Syntax Tricks

There are many more search syntax tricks; I won't expand on them here. You can refer to [this post](https://www.w3h5.com/post/220.html) (Chinese) to learn more. That said, mastering just a few of the common ones will already boost your search efficiency enormously.

PS: most search engines today are **keyword-based** rather than semantic, so please don't type "how do I find the book Learn Python the Hard Way" 🙃🙃🙃 — split it into keywords instead: `Learn Python the Hard Way book`, or even just `Learn Python Hard Way pdf`.

### 3.2 The "Mastering PDFs" Series

From **finding books** to **processing PDFs** to **choosing a reader on each platform** — this series is all you need.

Why does the "Mastering PDFs" series exist?

First, with the rise of the iPad and Surface, more and more people are choosing digital learning. But many either can't find digital learning materials, or the materials they find are low quality — which badly **undermines the efficiency of digital learning**.

Second, PDF has **full cross-platform compatibility**: on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, or iOS, a PDF renders identically. That property has also made PDF the publishing format of choice for the vast majority of textbooks, reports, and articles. Yet many people know very little about PDF and don't know how to handle common PDF-related problems.
