# The Idea

Compared with textbooks — which are mostly PDFs from relatively fixed sources — ordinary (non-textbook) books come in a much richer variety of formats and sources.

But each site's catalog is incomplete in its own way. Finding an ordinary book is really just a matter of switching the search across several sites and getting to the target book as fast as possible. So the factors that decide **how quickly you find a book** come down to two:

-   Does the site you choose have a complete-enough catalog?
    -   Can you switch between sites quickly — or even search the same keyword on multiple sites at once?

I'll offer my usual solutions along exactly those two lines.

One thing first, though. Unless you have a special need, or the stores simply don't carry the book, it's better to just buy it from an ebook store. As for why,

> Sometimes, free is the most expensive.

If you get it, you get it; if you don't, I won't say more.

# The Methods

## Douban Download Master

### A demo

This is the method I use most often — **efficient and fast**. The drawback: for now it works only on a computer.

See the right half of the image below for the demo.

![](/migrated-images/anki_20190714_04n6KC1mLfXk.png)

Open a book's page on Douban (China's main social platform for rating and reviewing books, movies, and music), and Douban Download Master queries that book across multiple sites simultaneously and returns the results. You can choose to hide the failed ones.

Just click into one successful result and you can download.

At its core, Douban Download Master is a small search engine of the **one input, many simultaneous searches** kind.

The official feature list:

> \*Show the corresponding Chinese and English subtitles  
> \*Show the corresponding online video | online audio | online books  
> \*Support searching Doulists (Douban's user-curated lists)  
> \*Show the corresponding movies | TV series | documentaries | variety shows | anime | music | books and other resources (direct links | FTP | BT | magnets | ED2K | NZB | Thunder | Baidu Pan | 115 Pan, etc.)  
> \*Aggregate 400+ mainstream resource sites | subtitle sites | resource search engines  
> \*Exclusive automatic resource color-marking: sites with resources show green, unreachable | not-logged-in sites show white, sites without resources show yellow  
> \*One-click generation of movie intro blurbs, handy for posting resources on forums | websites  
> \*Exclusive smart resource-ID matching for more precise results: with an IMDB link it matches by IMDB ID; without one, by Douban ID  
> \*Exclusive category-aware smart site display for more precise results: e.g., TV-series sites appear only on TV-series entries, anime sites only on anime entries  
> \*Exclusive country-aware smart site display for more precise results: e.g., American-drama sites appear only on American-drama entries, Japanese-drama sites only on Japanese-drama entries  
> \*Exclusive year-aware smart resource matching for more precise results: for movies the year is smartly appended to the keywords; for non-movies (series, anime, etc.) it isn't  
> \*Exclusive search-result caching mechanism: the more you use it, the faster it searches  
> \*Exclusive custom site removal plus custom resource-color background/foreground settings  
> \*Exclusive resource marking + site login + IMDB TOP 250 caching, with manual/automatic cache clearing  
> \*Exclusive MPAA rating notes in Chinese  
> \*Exclusive TV rating notes in Chinese  
> \*Exclusive production budget, domestic opening-week box office, North American opening-week box office, total box office  
> \*Show IMDB score and vote count; add a distinctive eye-catching mark to IMDB Top 250 titles  
> \*Show Metascore and popularity, plus this week's rise/fall  
> \*Show Rotten Tomatoes score, freshness, and spoilage  
> \*Show AniDB | BGm | MAL scores

## Chongbuluo Book Search

A while back, Chongbuluo (literally "Bug Tribe" — a well-known Chinese community for search techniques) aggregated a book-search directory based on the many find-a-book posts out there. The sites it lists may not be numerous, but they are the ones with **high search efficiency and fairly complete catalogs**. If Douban Download Master doesn't work for you, give Chongbuluo's book-search directory a try.
